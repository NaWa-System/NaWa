================================================================================
EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
================================================================================

This quarterly credit risk report provides a comprehensive assessment of Balticum Bank AG's loan portfolio quality as of December 2024. The bank maintains a total loan book of EUR 39.9 million across 15 active facilities, with the portfolio predominantly weighted toward secured residential mortgages which represent approximately 77% of total exposure.

Overall portfolio quality remains sound, with 13 facilities classified as performing and two exposures requiring elevated monitoring. The quarter saw stable credit metrics for the majority of the book, with Nordic mortgage exposures continuing to demonstrate strong repayment behavior.

However, the Baltic commercial segment presented challenges:
  - Riga Logistics SIA (Latvia): Deteriorated to Stage 3, 92 days past due
  - Baltic Food Distributors AS (Estonia): Watch List Stage 2, 18 days past due

Total loan loss provisions stood at EUR 103,600 at quarter end, representing adequate coverage of 24% against identified problem exposures.
