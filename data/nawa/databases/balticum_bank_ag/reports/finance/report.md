# Quarterly Financial Performance Report Q4 2024

**Organisation:** Balticum Bank AG  
**Reporting Period:** Q4 2024 (October - December 2024)  
**Report Date:** January 2025  

---

## Executive Summary

Balticum Bank AG delivered a stable financial performance in the fourth quarter of 2024, with continued growth in fee-based income streams offsetting modest pressure on net interest margins. The bank's lending portfolio remained healthy, with total outstanding loans of approximately EUR 40 million across retail and commercial segments, though isolated credit quality concerns in the Baltic SME segment require ongoing attention.

Fee income performance was particularly strong, driven by robust foreign exchange activity from corporate clients and steady advisory fee contributions from our wealth management division. Total fee income for the quarter exceeded EUR 10,000, representing a well-diversified revenue stream across transaction fees, account maintenance, and advisory services.

Digital channel adoption showed continued momentum, with mobile and online banking channels accounting for the majority of customer transaction activity. Looking ahead to 2025, management remains focused on prudent credit risk management while pursuing selective growth opportunities.

## Net Interest Income Analysis

The bank's lending portfolio generated stable interest income during Q4 2024, with the mortgage book representing the largest contributor at a weighted average rate of approximately 4.1%. Commercial lending products provided higher margin contributions at rates between 4.5% and 6.9%. The interest rate environment remained supportive, though competitive pressure on mortgage pricing continued in the Swedish market.

---

## Fee Income Performance by Category

Fee-based revenue demonstrated strong diversification in Q4 2024:

- **Transaction Fees:** EUR 8,940 (wire transfers, FX commissions)
- **Advisory Fees:** EUR 925 (wealth management services)
- **Account & Card Fees:** EUR 981 (maintenance, annual fees)

Corporate clients Suomi Marine Exports Oy and Scandinavian Timber Group AB were significant contributors through regular FX and international payment activity.

---

## Deposit Base and Liquidity Position

Total customer deposits stood at approximately EUR 15.7 million at quarter end. The deposit mix remained healthy, with:

- Current accounts representing operational balances
- Savings and term deposits providing stable funding
- Premium and Affluent segments maintaining largest concentrations
- Geographic strength in Sweden and Finland

---

## Transaction Activity by Channel

Digital channels dominated transaction activity:

- **Mobile App & Online Banking:** 68% of transactions
- **Branch-Assisted:** 20% (primarily high-value corporate)
- **ATM & POS:** 12% (retail banking patterns)

The Helsinki and Stockholm flagship locations supported major corporate client activity requiring complex processing.

---

## Geographic Revenue Distribution

- **Sweden:** Dominant contributor via mortgage book and corporate relationships
- **Finland:** Strong corporate banking, significant FX activity
- **Estonia:** Growth potential through digital-native SME clients
- **Latvia:** Mixed performance, isolated credit concerns
