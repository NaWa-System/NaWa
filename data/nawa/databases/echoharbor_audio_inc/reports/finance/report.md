## Executive Summary
This Q1 2026 royalty snapshot summarizes monthly royalty expense recognized for key artists on EchoHarbor Audio, using the platform’s per-stream rate and monthly stream totals to estimate payout obligations. Across the quarter, total royalties increased month-over-month, consistent with rising total stream counts and listening minutes reported in the monthly statements.

The expense distribution is meaningfully concentrated in the highest-volume artist segments, with major-label catalog commanding the largest single share of total royalty outlay, while indie and independent-label artists collectively represent a broad long-tail of smaller but growing obligations. This concentration suggests that shifts in a small number of high-volume catalogs can materially impact quarterly royalty expense.

Operationally, the consistent application of rate-per-stream by artist-month supports predictable forecasting, but the current view highlights the need to reconcile with revenue and margin performance (e.g., premium subscription collections and ad revenue) to assess contribution margin by region and plan. As EchoHarbor heads into Q2, priority focus areas should include improving forecasting accuracy (linking streams to plan mix and region) and monitoring rate shifts or contractual changes that could affect per-stream economics.
