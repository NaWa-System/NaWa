# ICA Handlarnas AB
## Wholesale Sales Performance Report — Q4 2024
**Organisation:** ICA Handlarnas AB  
**Reporting Period:** October – December 2024  
**Prepared by:** Business Intelligence & Reporting Division  
**Date:** January 2025

---

## Executive Summary

This report covers the wholesale supply performance of ICA Handlarnas AB for Q4 2024. The quarter delivered consistent month-over-month growth, with December recording the highest aggregate wholesale volume driven by pre-holiday stock builds at larger-format stores.

Across eight active stores, total wholesale disbursements grew from approximately SEK 485,000 in October to over SEK 629,000 in December — a growth of roughly 30%. ICA Maxi format stores (Kungens Kurva and Uppsala) were the dominant volume contributors throughout the quarter.

Private-label product lines (ICA Basic, ICA Selection) featured in the majority of order compositions, reflecting continued progress toward a higher-margin product mix within the cooperative supply chain.

One order from ICA Kvantum Örebro (December 23) remained in Pending status at period close due to a logistics scheduling issue. Resolution is expected in early January 2025.

## Report Sections

### 1. Monthly Wholesale Volume Trend

October wholesale orders totalled approximately SEK 485,000 across the network. November grew modestly to around SEK 506,000 as stores began building pre-holiday stock positions. December accelerated significantly to approximately SEK 630,000 — the highest monthly figure in the tracked period. This seasonal pattern is consistent with Swedish grocery retail dynamics, where the weeks before Christmas drive the largest replenishment cycles.

### 2. Revenue Contribution by Store Format

ICA Maxi stores (Kungens Kurva and Uppsala) collectively generated the largest wholesale revenue share, reflecting their scale and the breadth of products they carry. ICA Kvantum locations formed the second tier (SEK 60,000–95,000 per order), followed by ICA Supermarket locations in a moderate range. ICA Nära convenience stores recorded the smallest per-order values, consistent with their limited assortment.

### 3. Top Ordered Products by Volume

Arla Mellanmjölk 3L, Scan Kycklingfilé 1kg, and Coca-Cola 1.5L were among the most consistently ordered products across the quarter. ICA private-label items — particularly ICA Basic Diskmedel and ICA Basic Frysta Ärtor — featured in repeat orders from mid-size format stores. ICA Selection Lax showed elevated ordering frequency in December, likely reflecting holiday demand.

### 4. Private-Label vs Branded Order Composition

Private-label SKUs (ICA Basic and ICA Selection) were present in 18 of 24 orders, demonstrating strong network-wide adoption. These products carry higher wholesale margins than branded equivalents, meaning continued private-label penetration improves the cooperative's margin profile structurally. The ICA Selection fresh category showed particularly strong representation in December.

### 5. Regional Performance Overview

The Stockholm region led total wholesale contribution, anchored by ICA Maxi Kungens Kurva. Uppsala and Västra Götaland (Gothenburg) followed as the next strongest regions. Skåne, Östergötland, and Örebro contributed stable but smaller volumes, consistent with single-store representation in those regions.

### 6. Order Fulfilment & Delivery Timeliness

23 of 24 orders were delivered with an average lead time of approximately two calendar days, within the standard service-level target. One order remains pending due to Christmas-period logistics constraints and is expected to be resolved in January 2025.

## Appendix

### Assumptions
- Revenue figures reflect wholesale charges to store operators, not end-consumer retail sales.
- Private-label identification is based on the product category flag in the database.
- Month-over-month growth excludes the pending order.

### Known Limitations
- End-consumer sales data is not included; this report covers wholesale supply activity only.
- Only 8 of approximately 1,300 ICA network stores are represented.
- Pending December order means Q4 totals are slightly understated.
