# ICA Handlarnas AB
## Loyalty Programme Activity Report — Q4 2024
**Organisation:** ICA Handlarnas AB  
**Reporting Period:** October – December 2024  
**Prepared by:** Customer Insights & Loyalty Analytics  
**Date:** January 2025

---

## Executive Summary

This report summarises loyalty programme activity across the ICA Handlarnas AB store network for Q4 2024. The programme currently has 10 tracked members distributed across eight stores, with four Gold, three Silver, and three Bronze tier members.

Gold-tier members were disproportionately responsible for total loyalty spend and points earned. Members associated with ICA Maxi Uppsala and ICA Kvantum Gothenburg City recorded the highest individual spend figures. December saw the highest concentration of loyalty transactions, consistent with the seasonal peak observed in the wholesale performance report.

Bronze-tier members showed limited Q4 activity, suggesting a potential engagement gap that warrants targeted campaign activity in Q1 2025.

## Report Sections

### 1. Loyalty Member Tier Distribution

Of the 10 tracked members, four hold Gold status, three Silver, and three Bronze. Gold members represent 40% of the base but contribute a substantially higher share of loyalty spend. The three Bronze-tier members (4, 7, 9) joined more recently (2023) and hold lower accumulated point balances.

### 2. Transaction Volume and Spend by Month

October recorded 10 transactions totalling approximately SEK 4,391. November saw 4 transactions and around SEK 2,820 in spend. December showed a clear uplift with 6 transactions generating approximately SEK 5,182, consistent with the seasonal shopping peak.

### 3. Member Spend by Home Store

Members at ICA Maxi Uppsala and ICA Kvantum Gothenburg City recorded the highest cumulative spend, reflecting both large-format basket sizes and Gold-tier engagement levels. ICA Nära home-store members recorded the lowest totals, consistent with the convenience format's lower average basket.

### 4. Points Accumulation and Tier Progression Risk

Gold members hold strong balances (18,400–41,200 points). Silver members are stable but should be monitored for tier retention. Bronze members hold low balances (1,800–3,100 points) and showed limited Q4 activity — targeted promotions are recommended ahead of the next tier review.

### 5. Cross-Store Shopping Behaviour

Most transactions were recorded at the member's home store. A small number of cross-store transactions were observed, indicating some catchment overlap. Tracking this behaviour can support format-level marketing decisions.

## Appendix

### Assumptions
- 1 point per SEK spent, rounded to nearest integer.
- Tier classifications reflect cumulative programme history.
- Monthly totals derived from transaction_date grouping.

### Known Limitations
- 10-member sample; not representative of full ICA loyalty membership.
- Points redemption not tracked; total_points is gross accumulation only.
- Tier thresholds not defined in the schema.

### Recommended Next Analysis
- Add points redemption table to track net points liability.
- Analyse tier upgrade/downgrade velocity for at-risk Silver members.
- Measure impact of Bronze re-engagement campaigns in Q1 2025.
- Correlate loyalty spend with wholesale order volumes at store level.
