EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
-----------------

Verdant Shelf Co. delivered a strong first quarter in 2024, recording 20 orders
across its online and in-store channels with gross product revenue approaching
$1,980 before discounts and the single refund recorded in late March. The quarter
demonstrated healthy month-over-month volume growth, with March emerging as the
highest-order-count month driven by the spring seasonal uplift and increased
returning customer activity.

Tropical Plants and Accessories & Care remained the top-performing categories by
revenue contribution, reflecting the core customer preference for statement plants
paired with premium care products. The Online channel dominated order volume at
approximately 75% of transactions, while in-store orders carried higher average
basket values — particularly for large-format Tropical Plant SKUs.

One order was refunded during the period (Order #20, March 30), representing less
than 5% of total orders. Discounts were applied selectively across six orders,
indicating disciplined promotional activity. The spring season ahead presents a
clear growth opportunity, and Verdant Shelf is well positioned to capitalise on
increased consumer demand in Q2.

SECTION 1 — MONTHLY ORDER VOLUME TREND
================================================================================

Order volume grew consistently across the quarter: January recorded 5 orders,
February 6, and March delivered the strongest performance at 9 orders. The March
spike aligns with the traditional spring houseplant buying season and coincides
with the Spring Potting Party workshop event held on March 9, which likely drove
both footfall and online discovery.

The upward trend is encouraging and suggests natural seasonal tailwinds as the
company enters Q2. Management should ensure inventory adequacy for Tropical Plants
and growing kits ahead of April.

  - Line chart overlaying order count with workshop event dates


================================================================================
SECTION 2 — REVENUE BY PRODUCT CATEGORY
================================================================================

Tropical Plants generated the highest aggregate revenue across the quarter, driven
by the high unit prices of the Monstera Deliciosa, Fiddle Leaf Fig, and Bird of
Paradise. Accessories & Care performed strongly as a secondary category, with the
Ceramic Pot Set (3-pack) and Premium Potting Mix regularly appearing as add-on
items alongside plant purchases — a clear cross-sell pattern.

Succulents & Cacti and Ferns & Foliage delivered moderate revenue contributions.
Growing Kits showed consistent but modest performance, suggesting potential for
bundle promotion to improve attach rate and basket size.


  - Horizontal bar chart of revenue by category
  - Pie chart of category revenue share


================================================================================
SECTION 3 — CHANNEL PERFORMANCE: ONLINE VS. IN-STORE
================================================================================

The Online channel processed 75% of all orders, confirming its role as the
primary revenue engine. However, in-store orders consistently featured higher-
priced items — most notably the Bird of Paradise ($74.99) and Boston Fern with
add-on Potting Mix — resulting in above-average basket sizes.

In-store orders also carried zero shipping cost, improving net margin per
transaction. The in-store channel additionally functions as a physical gateway to
workshop events, creating a dual commercial role disproportionate to its raw order
count. The company should consider strategies to convert in-store browsers into
online repeat purchasers via loyalty or subscription upsell programmes.


  - Grouped bar chart: order count and AOV by channel
  - Comparative table: channel revenue, discounts applied, and shipping cost


================================================================================
SECTION 4 — TOP PRODUCTS BY UNITS SOLD AND REVENUE
================================================================================

The Monstera Deliciosa (Medium) was the most frequently ordered individual product,
appearing in three separate orders. The Ceramic Pot Set ranked highly by units,
reflecting strong accessory attach rates across the range. The Bird of Paradise
contributed the highest per-unit revenue at $74.99.

The Barrel Cactus was ordered in a quantity of 3 in a single transaction,
suggesting gift or bulk purchasing behaviour. Terrarium Starter Kits and Herb Grow
Kits show solid quantities per order, appealing to gift buyers and DIY enthusiasts.
No products were out of stock during the period; however, high-velocity SKUs
should be flagged for Q2 reorder planning.


  - Ranked bar chart: top 10 products by units sold
  - Scatter plot: unit price vs. units sold, coloured by category


================================================================================
SECTION 5 — DISCOUNT AND PROMOTIONS ANALYSIS
================================================================================

Six orders received discounts totalling $33.00 — approximately 1.7% of gross
product revenue. The largest single discount applied was $10.00 (Order #4) and
the average per discounted order was $5.50. Discounting was distributed across
both online and in-store channels with no apparent over-concentration in either.

The relatively low rate of discounting is positive and suggests that demand is
being captured at full price for the majority of transactions. The company should
monitor whether discount usage increases during Q2 as competition intensifies
during peak spring season.

  - Bar chart: discount amount by month

tickets (87% average capacity utilisation). Total event revenue reached
approximately $3,155.

================================================================================
SECTION 7 — CUSTOMER ACQUISITION AND REPEAT PURCHASE BEHAVIOUR
================================================================================

Fifteen unique customers transacted during Q1 2024, with five placing more than
one order across the quarter — a repeat rate of approximately 33%. Returning
customers included Ava Lindqvist, Marcus Osei, Priya Nair, Ethan Cho, and Naomi
Adeyemi, all of whom placed at least two purchases within the 90-day window.

The majority of new customers were acquired through the Online channel (10 of 15),
while in-store acquisitions skewed toward Austin-based buyers. Geographic spread
is widening to include Denver, Portland, New Orleans, and Dallas — an early
indicator of organic national reach through the e-commerce channel.
