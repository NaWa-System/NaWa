EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
-----------------

Verdant Shelf Co.'s subscription box programme ended Q1 2024 with 9 active
subscribers across three plan tiers — Sprout ($24.99/month), Grow ($44.99/month),
and Flourish ($69.99/month). The programme acquired 11 subscribers during the
quarter and experienced 2 cancellations, yielding a Q1 cohort churn rate of
approximately 18%.

Estimated monthly recurring revenue (MRR) at quarter-end is approximately $389 to
$465, with an annualised subscription run rate approaching $4,670. The Grow plan
proved the most popular tier by active subscriber count, while Flourish delivered
the highest revenue per subscriber at $69.99/month — confirming that the premium
tier resonates with the company's engaged plant enthusiast audience.

Both cancellations originated from the January cohort, signalling that first-box
experience and onboarding quality are critical early retention levers. Subscriber
acquisition accelerated through the quarter, with six of eleven sign-ups occurring
after January, indicating growing digital discovery and word-of-mouth momentum.

SECTION 5 — SUBSCRIPTION REVENUE VS. ONE-TIME ORDER REVENUE
================================================================================

Subscription MRR at the end of Q1 (~$389–$465/month) represents a growing
but still modest proportion of total company revenue relative to Q1 gross product
sales of approximately $1,980. However, the subscription channel provides a
predictable, recurring revenue base that is structurally more valuable than an
equivalent volume of one-time order revenue, as it reduces monthly revenue
volatility and improves cash flow predictability.

Importantly, several subscribers — including Ava Lindqvist, Priya Nair, and
Naomi Adeyemi — also placed one-time product orders during the quarter. This
confirms that the subscription product is driving incremental spending rather
than cannibalising product sales, a critical validation of the dual-revenue model.

SECTION 6 — RETENTION RATE AND FORWARD OUTLOOK
================================================================================

Early cohort retention results for Q1 2024:

  January Cohort (5 subscribers):   3 active at end of March (~60% retained)
  February Cohort (3 subscribers):  3 active at end of March (100% retained)
  March Cohort (3 subscribers):     3 active at end of March (100% — too early
                                    to assess meaningful retention)

These results are broadly consistent with industry benchmarks for new subscription
box services in the lifestyle category, but the sample size is small. The company
should target a 70%+ 90-day retention rate as the operational benchmark for Q2.

Key retention levers recommended for Q2:
  - Personalised plant recommendations tailored to each subscriber's history
  - A post-delivery care guide included with every box
  - A loyalty incentive (discount or bonus plant) for subscribers active beyond
    three consecutive months

APPENDIX
================================================================================

ASSUMPTIONS
  - MRR is calculated as the sum of monthly_price for all subscriptions with
    status = 'Active' as of March 31, 2024.
  - Churn rate is defined as cancellations during the quarter divided by total
    subscribers acquired during the quarter (not a start-of-period base, as the
    programme launched in Q1).
  - ARPU is estimated end-of-Q1 MRR divided by 9 active subscribers.
  - Subscription revenue is inferred from plan pricing and status; no billing
    table exists in the schema to confirm actual cash collected.
  - Subscribers with both a subscription and product orders are counted in both
    reports without double-counting their individual spend contributions.

KNOWN LIMITATIONS
  - No subscription billing or invoice table exists; actual cash collected cannot
    be precisely reconciled from the current schema.
  - Box contents per shipment are not tracked; subscription-specific COGS and
    gross margin cannot be calculated.
  - No customer satisfaction, NPS, or first-box rating data is captured.
  - Only Active and Cancelled statuses are available; paused or skipped
    subscriptions are not represented in the current data model.
  - The subscriber base is small (11 total, 9 active); all conclusions should be
    treated as directional, not statistically definitive.

RECOMMENDED NEXT ANALYSIS
  - Introduce a subscription_invoices table with payment dates and amounts billed
    to enable accurate MRR, ARR, and payment reconciliation.
  - Add an exit_survey_responses table to capture structured cancellation reasons
    and begin building a churn prediction capability.
  - Track box contents per subscription shipment to calculate subscription-
    specific COGS and gross margin by plan tier.
  - Build a subscriber lifetime value (sLTV) model once a minimum of 6 months
    of cohort data is available.
  - Design a cross-sell analysis to quantify how subscription membership
    influences incremental one-time product order spending per customer.
