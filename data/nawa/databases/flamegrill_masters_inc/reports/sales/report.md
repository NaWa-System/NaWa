# Q1 2024 Sales and Product Performance Analysis
Organisation: FlameGrill Masters Inc.
Reporting Period: Q1 2024

## Executive Summary
This report outlines the sales and product performance for FlameGrill Masters Inc. during the first quarter of 2024. The analysis is derived from transactional data across our primary locations, identifying key revenue drivers and popular menu items. Overall, Q1 saw steady demand, with the Flame Classic Burger maintaining its position as the top-selling product. Furthermore, meal combinations involving sides and beverages contributed significantly to the average order value.

## Key Metrics
- Total Q1 Revenue: $108.50 (Cumulative revenue generated across all orders)
- Total Orders: 8 (Total number of transactions completed)
- Average Order Value (AOV): $13.56
- Top Selling Product: Flame Classic Burger (7 units sold)
- Highest Grossing Location: Highway 99 Drive-Thru ($56.50)

## Main Sections

### Revenue by Location
An analysis of Q1 sales indicates that the Highway 99 Drive-Thru slightly outperformed the Metro Center location in total revenue, driven by higher-value evening orders.

### Product Popularity and Mix
The Flame Classic Burger remains the highest volume item, though the Double Flame Burger drives significant revenue due to its higher price point. Sides, particularly Crispy Fries, have a strong attach rate to burger purchases.

### Sales Trend Over Time
Monthly order volume remained relatively consistent throughout the quarter, with March seeing the highest number of individual transactions across both sites.

### Average Order Value Analysis
AOV remains healthy at $13.56, suggesting that most customers are ordering a main item paired with a side or beverage, aligning with our promotional combo strategies.

## Appendix & Notes
Assumptions:
- All recorded orders in the database were successfully fulfilled and paid.
- Returns or refunds are assumed to be negligible.

Known Limitations:
- The current dataset only encompasses a limited timeframe (Q1 2024), preventing year-over-year comparisons.
