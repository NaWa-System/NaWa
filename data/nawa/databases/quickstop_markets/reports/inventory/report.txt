This report presents the end-of-quarter inventory position across five QuickStop Markets locations as of March 31, 2024, identifying stock levels relative to established reorder thresholds and flagging products at immediate or near-term replenishment risk. The snapshot captures 22 product-store combinations representing key SKUs across Beverages, Fresh Deli, Tobacco & Vape, Household Essentials, and Frozen & Dairy categories.

The most pressing replenishment concern is at Store 4 (Portland East), where Laundry Detergent Pod 10ct has a quantity-on-hand of just five units against a reorder level of ten — below threshold and requiring an immediate purchase order. Conversely, several locations maintain healthy or surplus inventory positions. Store 1's Bottled Water and Fountain Drink stocks are well above reorder thresholds, and Store 3's Cigarette stock at 62 units provides comfortable cover. These positions reduce short-term procurement urgency.

The report recommends immediate action on three at-risk SKUs, a review of reorder level parameters for high-velocity items, and a streamlined replenishment protocol for Stores 4 and 2.

APPENDIX AND NOTES
========================================

ASSUMPTIONS
- Inventory records represent end-of-quarter stock positions as of March 31, 2024; no intra-day movements after the final transaction are reflected.
- Reorder levels reflect thresholds set by the central procurement team and represent intended minimum stock positions before replenishment.
- Last_restocked_date represents the most recent full replenishment event; partial top-up orders may not be captured.
- No spoilage, damage, or shrinkage adjustments are reflected in quantity_on_hand figures.
- The inventory table covers a representative subset of the full product catalogue.

KNOWN LIMITATIONS
- No best-before or expiry date field exists in the inventory schema, limiting freshness analysis for perishable Fresh Deli and Frozen & Dairy items.
- No supplier lead time data is available, preventing calculation of days-of-stock-remaining relative to next expected delivery.
- Inventory coverage is incomplete; not all stores have all products represented in the inventory table.
- Sales velocity estimates rely on a transaction sample; full-quarter data would enable more accurate sell-through rate modelling.
- Shrinkage, theft, and waste are not tracked and may cause discrepancies between system-reported and physical stock figures.

RECOMMENDED NEXT ANALYSIS
- Integrate transaction_items sales velocity data with inventory records to calculate estimated days of stock remaining per SKU per store.
- Add supplier lead time data to enable delivery-window-adjusted automatic reorder point calculations.
- Introduce a product expiry or best-before date field to inventory records for Fresh Deli and Frozen & Dairy categories.
- Conduct a full reorder level audit for all SKUs across all stores, calibrated against Q1 observed sales velocity.
- Investigate Store 4 (Portland East) supply chain and logistics performance to determine whether repeated below-threshold positions reflect a structural gap.
- Develop a weekly automated inventory alert report flagging all below-threshold positions within 48 hours of data refresh.
