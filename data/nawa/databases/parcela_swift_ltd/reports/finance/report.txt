================================================================================
EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
================================================================================

Parcela Swift Ltd. delivered a financially strong first quarter in 2024, with
total network revenue growing consistently from January through to March across
all five depot locations. London Gateway Depot retained its position as the
primary revenue engine of the business, while Birmingham Central demonstrated the
most improved revenue-to-cost ratio across the quarter, driven by growing B2B
contract activity and efficient use of its owned Heavy Goods Vehicle asset. Every
depot recorded a positive gross contribution in each reporting month, confirming
that no part of the network was operating below cost recovery threshold during
the period.

Operating costs grew broadly in line with shipment volume increases, reflecting
the predominantly variable character of Parcela Swift's cost base. However, the
Dublin Express Depot continued to exhibit a structurally higher cost-to-revenue
ratio than its UK counterparts, a pattern attributable to its fully contracted
vehicle fleet and the administrative overhead associated with cross-border
logistics operations between Ireland and the UK. This dynamic is expected to
persist unless addressed through targeted pricing adjustments or a transition
toward partly owned fleet capacity in Dublin.

The Freight Pallet service tier generated the highest revenue per consignment
across the quarter, with individual bookings from CrestMed Pharmaceuticals and
Ferngrove Organics consistently exceeding GBP 95 per shipment. Two unresolved
Freight Pallet exception cases at quarter-end -- including one involving a
declared consignment value of GBP 4,500 -- represent a contingent financial
liability that should be estimated and provisioned before Q1 accounts are
finalised.

Heading into Q2, the revenue growth trajectory is encouraging. Management should
focus on sustaining the Birmingham Central margin improvement, addressing the
Dublin cost structure, and ensuring the two highest-exposure exception cases are
resolved promptly to protect the otherwise strong Q1 financial result.

SECTION 1: REVENUE PERFORMANCE BY DEPOT
================================================================================

London Gateway Depot generated the highest total revenue in each of the three
reporting months, underpinned by high shipment throughput and a favourable mix
of Next Day Express and Freight Pallet bookings from Halsworth Retail Group and
Consumer Direct Pool customers.

Birmingham Central recorded strong revenue performance, growing materially in
February and March as Voltline Tech Solutions activity increased alongside
CrestMed Pharmaceuticals' regular freight pallet schedule.

Manchester North showed consistent growth driven by Ferngrove Organics and
TinderBox Gifts Ltd. activity. Glasgow Hub and Dublin Express both recorded
month-on-month revenue increases across the quarter. Total network revenue grew
by approximately 25.3% between January and March, representing a positive
commercial trajectory entering Q2.

Data fields used: depot_performance.total_revenue_gbp, depot_performance.report_month,
depot_performance.depot_id, depots.depot_name, regions.region_name,
customers.customer_name

================================================================================
SECTION 2: OPERATING COST ANALYSIS BY DEPOT
================================================================================

Total operating costs across the network rose proportionally with shipment
volumes in Q1 2024, confirming the predominantly variable nature of Parcela
Swift's cost base. London Gateway incurred the highest absolute operating costs,
consistent with its position as the highest-volume depot.

Dublin Express exhibited the most unfavourable cost-to-revenue ratio throughout
the quarter, driven by its fully contracted vehicle fleet and the additional
overheads of cross-border logistics. Glasgow Hub carried elevated costs relative
to its revenue contribution in January, with the ratio improving as volumes grew
through February and March.

Birmingham Central demonstrated the most controlled cost management across Q1,
with operating cost growth lagging revenue growth in each successive month --
a sign of improving operational leverage at that location.

Data fields used: depot_performance.operating_cost_gbp,
depot_performance.total_revenue_gbp, depot_performance.report_month,
depots.depot_name, vehicles.owned_or_contracted

================================================================================
SECTION 3: GROSS MARGIN BY DEPOT AND MONTH
================================================================================

All five Parcela Swift depots recorded positive gross contributions in every
month of Q1 2024, confirming network-wide profitability above cost recovery
threshold throughout the reporting period. Network gross margin averaged 26.9%
before central overhead allocation.

London Gateway and Birmingham Central consistently outperformed this average,
benefitting from a combination of owned fleet assets and concentrated B2B
contract revenue. Manchester North performed at broadly the network average.

Glasgow Hub's gross margin was materially weaker in January but improved as
monthly volumes grew and fixed-like costs were spread across a higher shipment
base. Dublin Express remained the weakest-margin depot for the full quarter --
a structural rather than operational issue requiring a pricing or fleet strategy
response.

Data fields used: depot_performance.total_revenue_gbp,
depot_performance.operating_cost_gbp, depot_performance.depot_id,
depot_performance.report_month, depots.depot_name

================================================================================
SECTION 4: REVENUE BY SERVICE TYPE
================================================================================

Freight Pallet bookings contributed the highest revenue per individual
consignment across Q1, with individual transactions recorded between GBP 95 and
GBP 120 in the shipments dataset. Next Day Express generated the highest
cumulative revenue contribution by volume, reflecting both its premium base rate
and its dominance in the B2B shipment mix.

Two Day Standard provided a reliable mid-range revenue stream across multiple
accounts. Economy Parcel, most commonly used by the Consumer Direct Pool,
generated the lowest per-shipment yield at approximately GBP 5 per booking.

Returns Management revenue remained modest in absolute terms but showed a clear
upward trend linked to Halsworth Retail Group's growing returns volumes,
suggesting this service line warrants a dedicated pricing and capacity review
ahead of the post-Easter returns peak.

Data fields used: shipments.revenue_gbp, shipments.service_id,
service_types.service_name, service_types.base_rate_gbp, shipments.shipment_date,
customers.customer_name

================================================================================
SECTION 5: KEY ACCOUNT REVENUE CONTRIBUTION
================================================================================

Halsworth Retail Group was the most active account by shipment count across Q1,
generating revenue across three distinct service types -- Next Day Express,
Freight Pallet, and Returns Management -- indicating strong commercial dependency
on Parcela Swift's full service range.

CrestMed Pharmaceuticals, while operating a lower shipment frequency, contributed
significantly to revenue through high-value freight pallet bookings with declared
values consistently exceeding GBP 2,000 per consignment.

Ferngrove Organics submitted a high-value freight pallet booking in March valued
at GBP 120 in delivery revenue. Voltline Tech Solutions and TinderBox Gifts Ltd.
represent mid-tier accounts with growing activity trajectories.

The Consumer Direct Pool generated consistent but low-yield B2C volume across
Economy Parcel and Next Day Express services.

Data fields used: customers.customer_name, customers.customer_type,
shipments.revenue_gbp, shipments.service_id, shipments.customer_id,
shipments.shipment_date, shipments.declared_value_gbp

================================================================================
SECTION 6: EXCEPTION-LINKED FINANCIAL RISK AND PROVISIONING
================================================================================

Two unresolved delivery exceptions as of 31 March 2024 carry direct financial
implications requiring provisioning before Q1 accounts are closed.

  Shipment 1027 -- CrestMed Pharmaceuticals
    Freight pallet with declared value of GBP 4,500. Failed delivery caused by
    vehicle breakdown at Birmingham Central Depot on 14 March 2024. Claims review
    is pending. Depending on the outcome, Parcela Swift may face partial or full
    compensation under its freight liability policy. Finance should establish a
    minimum provision based on applicable policy liability cap.

  Shipment 1019 -- Cross-Border Economy Parcel
    Awaiting customs clearance documentation as of 31 March 2024. Holding and
    handling costs are accruing daily. A delay compensation request from the
    originating customer is possible if clearance is not obtained promptly.

Additionally, shipment 1012 (resolved in February) required a replacement
shipment dispatched at no charge following transit damage -- a direct revenue
cost to be formally recorded against the Birmingham Central depot P&L for Q1.

Finance is recommended to establish a minimum claims provision ahead of Q1
account finalisation.

Data fields used: delivery_exceptions.exception_type, delivery_exceptions.resolved,
delivery_exceptions.resolution_notes, shipments.declared_value_gbp,
shipments.revenue_gbp, shipments.status, customers.customer_name, depots.depot_name

APPENDIX
================================================================================

ASSUMPTIONS
  - depot_performance.total_revenue_gbp and operating_cost_gbp are treated as
    the authoritative financial figures for depot-level monthly reporting.
  - Gross margin is calculated as (revenue minus operating cost) divided by
    revenue, before any allocation of central overhead including corporate
    administration, insurance, IT, and senior management costs.
  - Individual shipment revenue figures in the shipments table are used for
    service-type and account-level analysis only.
  - Claims liability exposure is estimated based on shipments.declared_value_gbp;
    actual liability will depend on freight policy terms, claim investigation
    outcome, and applicable liability caps.

KNOWN LIMITATIONS
  - Central overhead cost allocations are absent from the schema; reported gross
    margin figures do not represent net operating margin.
  - Fuel expenditure, driver wages, and vehicle maintenance costs are not
    itemised; operating cost is a single monthly aggregate per depot.
  - Invoice settlement dates and accounts receivable ageing data are not
    available; cash flow and debtor reporting cannot be produced.
  - The impact of GBP/EUR exchange rate movements on the Dublin Express Depot
    cost and revenue base is not modelled in the current dataset.

RECOMMENDED NEXT ANALYSIS
  - Net margin analysis incorporating central overhead allocation by depot
    revenue share to establish true depot-level profitability.
  - Accounts receivable ageing report for all B2B clients, with priority focus
    on CrestMed Pharmaceuticals given the open exception and high declared values.
  - Freight liability claims register to formally track compensation exposure,
    provisioning levels, and settlement outcomes across all depots.
  - Dublin Express depot pricing review to assess whether current service rates
    for cross-border consignments adequately reflect the structural cost
    differential versus UK-based depots.
  - Returns Management service line profitability review ahead of anticipated
    volume growth in Q2 from Halsworth Retail Group.
