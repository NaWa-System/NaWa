================================================================================
EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
================================================================================

Parcela Swift Ltd. recorded a broadly positive first quarter in 2024, with total
shipment volumes growing month-on-month across all five depots and overall
on-time delivery performance remaining above the company's internal SLA benchmark
for the majority of the period. The London Gateway Depot continued to lead the
network in absolute shipment volumes, while the Dublin Express Depot demonstrated
meaningful improvement in delivery reliability as the quarter progressed.

The quarter was nonetheless marked by operational challenges that warrant
management attention. Eight delivery exceptions were logged across the network,
spanning causes including vehicle breakdown, sorting errors, recipient
unavailability, and an ongoing cross-border customs delay. As of the reporting
date, four exceptions remain unresolved, representing a residual risk requiring
active case management from respective depot supervisors before Q2 commences.

The Glasgow Hub recorded the weakest on-time delivery rate relative to its volume
in January and February, with partial recovery observed in March. The Freight
Pallet service category generated the highest revenue per consignment across all
service tiers but also accounted for the two most commercially significant
unresolved exception cases at quarter-end.

Forward-looking, the month-on-month volume trajectory suggests that London
Gateway and Manchester North may face capacity pressure during Q2 peak weeks. A
structured capacity planning review is recommended ahead of the Easter and early
summer peak periods to avoid service quality deterioration.

SECTION 1: MONTHLY SHIPMENT VOLUME TRENDS
================================================================================

Shipment volumes across the Parcela Swift network increased consistently
throughout Q1 2024. London Gateway Depot processed the highest absolute volumes
each month, benefitting from its proximity to major retail and e-commerce clients
including Halsworth Retail Group. Birmingham Central and Manchester North depots
recorded steady mid-tier volumes, reflecting their established B2B contract bases.
Glasgow Hub and Dublin Express both demonstrated improving throughput as the
quarter progressed.

The network-wide increase from 310 shipments in January to 380 in March reflects
both organic expansion of existing accounts and the ramping activity of Voltline
Tech Solutions, onboarded at Birmingham Central in June 2023.

Data fields used: depot_performance.total_shipments, depot_performance.report_month,
depots.depot_name, shipments.shipment_date, customers.customer_name

================================================================================
SECTION 2: ON-TIME DELIVERY PERFORMANCE BY DEPOT
================================================================================

Network on-time delivery performance averaged 88.6% across Q1 2024, within
Parcela Swift's contracted SLA minimum of 87% for standard service tiers. London
Gateway maintained the strongest performance, sustaining an on-time rate above
91% across all three months.

The Glasgow Hub underperformed the network average in January at approximately
85.7%, recovering to 91.7% in February and continuing to improve through March.
The Dublin Express Depot achieved consistent on-time rates above 90% throughout
the period. Manchester North showed a marginal dip in on-time performance in
March, coinciding with the period in which two exception cases were logged at
that location.

Data fields used: depot_performance.on_time_deliveries,
depot_performance.total_shipments, depot_performance.failed_deliveries,
depots.depot_name, depot_performance.report_month

================================================================================
SECTION 3: DELIVERY EXCEPTION ANALYSIS
================================================================================

Eight delivery exceptions were recorded across the Parcela Swift network in Q1
2024. Exception types were as follows:

  - Missed Collection (resolved)
  - Address Not Found (resolved)
  - Recipient Unavailable (unresolved -- parcel held at depot)
  - Damaged in Transit (resolved -- replacement dispatched at no charge)
  - Customs Delay (unresolved -- awaiting clearance documentation)
  - Driver Route Deviation (resolved -- apology issued)
  - Vehicle Breakdown (unresolved -- ETA pending)
  - Sorting Error (unresolved -- investigation underway)

Highest-risk unresolved cases:
  Shipment 1027: CrestMed Pharmaceuticals freight pallet. Vehicle breakdown at
    Birmingham Central Depot on 14 March 2024. Customer notified. Resolution
    pending; potential freight liability claim.
  Shipment 1034: London Gateway sorting error logged 29 March 2024. Parcel
    mislabelled. Investigation underway.

The exception rate remains below 1% of total network shipments, within acceptable
operational parameters. However, the concentration of open cases in March warrants
close monitoring heading into Q2.

Data fields used: delivery_exceptions.exception_type, delivery_exceptions.resolved,
delivery_exceptions.resolution_notes, shipments.status, customers.customer_name

================================================================================
SECTION 4: SERVICE TYPE UTILISATION
================================================================================

Next Day Express was the most utilised service type by shipment count throughout
Q1, driven by time-sensitive B2B accounts in retail and technology. Two Day
Standard served as the preferred mid-tier option for clients where cost efficiency
takes precedence over speed, including TinderBox Gifts Ltd. and Ferngrove
Organics. Economy Parcel accounted for the majority of Consumer Direct Pool
shipments, consistent with cost-conscious B2C behaviour.

Freight Pallet, while lower in volume, generated approximately GBP 103 per
consignment on average -- the highest yield of any service tier. Returns
Management saw growing uptake from Halsworth Retail Group, suggesting that this
supplementary service may warrant further commercial development in Q2.

Data fields used: service_types.service_name, service_types.base_rate_gbp,
shipments.service_id, shipments.revenue_gbp, shipments.shipment_date

================================================================================
SECTION 5: CUSTOMER SHIPMENT ACTIVITY
================================================================================

Halsworth Retail Group and CrestMed Pharmaceuticals were the two highest-volume
B2B customers by both shipment count and revenue contribution across Q1 2024.
Halsworth utilised a broad service mix including Next Day Express, Freight Pallet,
and Returns Management, indicating strong account depth and dependency on Parcela
Swift's full service range.

CrestMed's freight pallet consignments carry the highest declared values of any
customer in the dataset, underscoring the commercial sensitivity of this account
and the importance of resolving the open exception case before Q2 billing.

TinderBox Gifts Ltd. experienced late deliveries on two occasions, both routed
through Manchester North Depot, which may indicate a collection scheduling
misalignment at that location requiring depot-level investigation.

Cairn Fine Foods maintained a low but consistent shipment profile with improving
on-time delivery rates in February and March following a single failed delivery
recorded in January.

Data fields used: customers.customer_name, customers.customer_type,
shipments.customer_id, shipments.status, shipments.revenue_gbp,
shipments.service_id, shipments.depot_id

================================================================================
SECTION 6: FLEET AND DEPOT CAPACITY OVERVIEW
================================================================================

Parcela Swift operates ten registered vehicles across five depots, split between
Transit Vans (800 kg capacity) and Heavy Goods Vehicles rated between 4,500 and
5,000 kg. London Gateway holds the largest fleet at three owned vehicles,
consistent with its role as the highest-throughput depot in the network.

Three of the ten fleet vehicles are contracted rather than owned, concentrated at
Birmingham Central, Manchester North, and across both Dublin Express positions.
The vehicle breakdown recorded in March at Birmingham involving a contracted
Transit Van highlights the operational risk inherent in contracted fleet
dependency, where Parcela Swift has limited direct oversight of maintenance
standards and scheduling.

A formal contracted fleet reliability audit is recommended as a Q2 priority
action, particularly given Birmingham Central's accelerating shipment volumes.

Data fields used: vehicles.vehicle_type, vehicles.capacity_kg,
vehicles.owned_or_contracted, vehicles.depot_id, depots.depot_name,
delivery_exceptions.exception_type, delivery_exceptions.resolved

APPENDIX
================================================================================

ASSUMPTIONS
  - depot_performance monthly figures are treated as authoritative aggregates for
    network-level reporting purposes.
  - On-time delivery is defined as actual_delivery_date <= promised_delivery_date.
  - Shipments with status 'In Transit' are excluded from on-time rate calculations
    but are included in total shipment volume counts.
  - The Consumer Direct Pool (customer_id 5) represents aggregated B2C demand
    and should not be interpreted as a single named commercial account.

KNOWN LIMITATIONS
  - The shipments table contains a transactional sample of 34 records; full
    operational volume for the quarter is reflected in the depot_performance
    aggregate table.
  - Fuel, driver labour, and vehicle maintenance costs are not individually
    captured; granular cost-per-delivery analysis is not possible from this data.
  - Cross-border shipment compliance data for Dublin is insufficient to support a
    full customs or regulatory performance review.
  - Vehicle utilisation rates against capacity cannot be calculated as trip-level
    load and mileage data are not currently recorded in the schema.

RECOMMENDED NEXT ANALYSIS
  - Root-cause investigation for late deliveries originating from Manchester
    North Depot, with focus on TinderBox Gifts Ltd. collection scheduling.
  - Capacity planning review for London Gateway and Manchester North ahead of Q2
    peak trading weeks.
  - Customer satisfaction and NPS scoring linked to shipment exception records.
  - Contracted versus owned vehicle reliability benchmarking including breakdown
    frequency, cost attribution, and SLA breach correlation.
  - Service type profitability modelling incorporating variable cost per kilogram
    once cost-per-shipment tracking is implemented across all depots.
