EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
========================================

This report provides an assessment of inventory health across FreshBasket Markets' five store locations as at the end of Q1 2024. The analysis draws on end-of-quarter stock snapshot data, product-level cost and pricing information, and transaction-level unit sales to evaluate stock adequacy, identify at-risk product lines, and support procurement planning for Q2 2024.

Overall, the majority of monitored stock-keeping units across stores maintained healthy quantities above their designated reorder thresholds at the close of the quarter. However, a number of items — most notably Dishwasher Tablets (30pk) across Beaverton and Bellevue stores — were identified as having fallen below their reorder levels, raising a moderate replenishment risk if sales velocity maintained its March trajectory.

Meat & Seafood products exhibited some of the tightest stock margins relative to reorder levels, particularly Atlantic Salmon Fillet at Portland Central and Seattle North. Given that this category is the highest revenue contributor and operates on a perishable handling cycle, maintaining adequate stock buffers is operationally critical.

Looking ahead to Q2, procurement planning should account for anticipated demand uplift driven by seasonal fresh produce availability, the continuation of promotional activity, and the expected growth in click-and-collect fulfilment at suburban locations.

ASSUMPTIONS
- Inventory data represents a point-in-time snapshot at end of Q1 2024 and does not reflect intra-quarter stock movements.
- Estimated stock value is calculated by multiplying inventory.stock_quantity by products.unit_cost, excluding shrinkage or pending deliveries.
- Reorder level thresholds are as recorded in the inventory table and assumed to reflect current operational policy per store.
- Sales velocity assessments are based on total Q1 transaction_items unit volumes.
- A standard 3-5 business day supplier lead time is assumed for replenishment urgency assessments.

KNOWN LIMITATIONS
- Only 23 product-store inventory records are available; the full range of store SKUs is broader than represented here.
- No intra-quarter stock movement records exist; temporary stockouts cannot be identified.
- Spoilage, waste, and shrinkage are not captured; perishable stock values may be slightly overstated.
- Supplier and purchase order data are not included in the current schema.
- No receiving records exist to calculate restocking volumes per delivery.

RECOMMENDED NEXT ANALYSIS
- Implement a stock movement log table to capture daily or weekly stock-in and stock-out events, enabling true velocity and turn rate calculations.
- Add supplier and purchase order tables to support full procurement lifecycle analysis including lead time performance.
- Develop a demand forecasting model using Q1 sales velocity adjusted for seasonal patterns and planned promotions.
- Conduct a spoilage and shrinkage audit for perishable categories to quantify wastage costs.
- Review and update reorder level thresholds for suburban stores to reflect growing click-and-collect demand.
