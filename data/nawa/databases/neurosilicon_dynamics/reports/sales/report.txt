Q1 2026 GPU and AI Accelerator Sales Performance Report

Organisation: NeuroSilicon Dynamics
Period: January 2026 - March 2026

Executive Summary
NeuroSilicon Dynamics demonstrated strong demand across both AI accelerators and GPU product lines. Growth was driven by cloud providers and OEM partners, particularly in North America and Europe.

Sales performance increased steadily throughout the quarter, with the NSD A100X leading revenue contributions. Inventory levels suggest potential supply constraints if demand continues at current levels.

Key Metrics
- Total Revenue: $2.8M+
- Top Product: NSD A100X
- Largest Customer Segment: Cloud Providers
- Average Order Size: High
- Inventory Utilization: Moderate

Revenue by Product Category
AI accelerators contributed the largest share of revenue, followed by GPUs and edge AI products.

Sales by Region
North America led total sales, with strong growth in Europe.

Customer Segment Performance
Cloud providers and OEMs generated the highest revenue contributions.

Order Trends Over Time
Order volume increased steadily throughout the quarter.

Inventory and Fulfillment
Inventory levels declined due to increased sales activity.
