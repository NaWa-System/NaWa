================================================================================
EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
================================================================================

MediPath Pharmacy Group's clinical services programme continued to expand its
reach during Q1 2024, with 20 service appointments recorded across all six
store locations. The programme delivered flu vaccinations, medication therapy
reviews, blood pressure checks, diabetes management reviews, and asthma
management consultations. The overall service completion rate was 95%, with only
one appointment cancelled during the period, reflecting strong operational
reliability across the clinical team.

Medication Therapy Reviews and Diabetes Management Reviews represented the
highest-fee clinical services, contributing meaningfully to supplementary revenue
beyond the core dispensing business. Flu vaccination services demonstrated high
volume throughput and served an important patient acquisition and community
health function.

From an inventory operations perspective, analysis of stock-on-hand levels
relative to reorder thresholds identified several locations approaching alert
status. The Spokane location's Atorvastatin 20mg inventory stood at 22 units
against a reorder level of 20. The Portland Main location's specialty compounding
product held only 18 units against a reorder threshold of 10, warranting close
monitoring given the product's high margin contribution and low substitutability.

Overall, the operational picture for Q1 2024 is positive, with clinical services
performing above minimum service volume targets and inventory management broadly
adequate. Proactive reorder processes should be initiated for at-risk products
ahead of Q2, and clinical service scheduling capacity should be reviewed in
preparation for anticipated spring demand increases.

SECTION 1: CLINICAL SERVICE VOLUME AND COMPLETION RATES
================================================================================

A total of 20 clinical service appointments were recorded across all six MediPath
locations during Q1 2024. Of these, 19 were completed successfully, yielding a
95% completion rate. The single cancellation occurred at MediPath Eugene in late
February and was recorded against a flu vaccination appointment. No no-show
records were captured in the current dataset. Service delivery was broadly
consistent across locations, with each store contributing between three and four
appointments during the quarter. The high completion rate reflects well on the
scheduling and clinical staffing model currently in operation.

Data fields used: clinical_services.service_id, clinical_services.store_id,
clinical_services.outcome_status, clinical_services.service_date,
clinical_services.service_type

================================================================================
SECTION 2: CLINICAL SERVICE REVENUE BY TYPE
================================================================================

Medication Therapy Reviews and Management Reviews generated the highest revenue
per appointment at $45.00 and $55.00 respectively, reflecting the specialist
pharmacist time and clinical complexity involved. Flu vaccinations were the most
frequently delivered service at $25.00 per appointment, contributing high volume
at moderate unit revenue. Blood pressure checks, at $15.00 per session, were the
lowest-fee service but provided a consistent and low-cost patient engagement
touchpoint. Collectively, clinical services generated $750.00 in gross fee
revenue during the quarter, representing a meaningful non-dispensing revenue
stream that complements the core pharmacy business.

Data fields used: clinical_services.service_type, clinical_services.service_fee,
clinical_services.outcome_status, clinical_services.service_date,
clinical_services.store_id

================================================================================
SECTION 3: CLINICAL SERVICE DISTRIBUTION BY STORE
================================================================================

MediPath Seattle Central conducted the highest number of clinical service events
in Q1, with four appointments across flu vaccination, medication therapy review,
and blood pressure check service types. MediPath Portland Main and MediPath
Bellevue each delivered three events, including higher-fee management review
services. MediPath Tacoma, Eugene, and Spokane each contributed three or four
events, demonstrating an operationally even distribution across the network.
Store-level variation in service type mix reflects local patient demographic
differences, with chronic disease management reviews more prevalent at stores
serving older patient populations.

Data fields used: clinical_services.store_id, clinical_services.service_type,
clinical_services.service_fee, clinical_services.service_date,
stores.store_name, stores.region

SECTION 4: PHARMACIST WORKLOAD AND SERVICE ATTRIBUTION
================================================================================

Clinical services were administered across six named pharmacists during Q1 2024,
with each pharmacist aligned to a primary store location. Pharmacist Chen at
Seattle Central and Pharmacist Ruiz at Portland Main each delivered multiple

Pharmacist Kim at Eugene recorded the only cancellation of the quarter. No

Data fields used: clinical_services.administered_by,
clinical_services.service_type, clinical_services.outcome_status,

================================================================================
SECTION 5: INVENTORY STOCK LEVEL ASSESSMENT
================================================================================

An assessment of current inventory levels relative to reorder thresholds across
all stores identified three products at elevated replenishment risk. At MediPath
Spokane, Atorvastatin 20mg 90ct had 22 units on hand against a reorder level of
20, placing it within one dispensing cycle of triggering a reorder alert. At
MediPath Portland Main, the Custom Compound Topical Pain Gel held 18 units
against a reorder level of 10; the specialised nature of this product and its
longer replenishment lead time warrant proactive ordering. At MediPath Tacoma,
Blood Glucose Test Strips held 35 units against a reorder level of 20, providing
a slightly more comfortable buffer. All other stocked products across the network
were observed to be holding inventory comfortably above their respective reorder
thresholds as of the most recent restock dates recorded.

Data fields used: inventory.store_id, inventory.product_id,
inventory.quantity_on_hand, inventory.reorder_level,
inventory.last_restocked_date, stores.store_name, products.product_name

APPENDIX
================================================================================

ASSUMPTIONS
- Clinical service fees are recorded as gross billed amounts; insurance rebates
  or patient subsidies applicable to vaccination services are not captured.
- 'Completed' outcome status indicates that the full service was delivered and
  recorded by the administering pharmacist.
- Inventory quantities represent physical stock on hand as of the
  last_restocked_date and do not account for dispensing between restock date
  and report generation date.
- Each pharmacist is assumed to be the primary clinical services administrator
  at their assigned store location during this period.

KNOWN LIMITATIONS
- The clinical_services table does not capture appointment booking lead time,
  patient wait times, or pharmacist hours consumed per service, limiting full
  workload analysis.
- Inventory data does not include pending purchase orders, in-transit stock, or
  historical consumption rates, which would be required for days-of-supply
  calculations.
- No data exists for supplier lead times or wholesale pricing, preventing full
  procurement cost analysis.
- The current dataset covers 12 customers across 6 stores and is illustrative
  in scale; real-world reporting would reflect substantially higher volumes.

RECOMMENDED NEXT ANALYSIS
- Implement a clinical service appointment booking and wait-time tracking module
  to enable capacity planning and patient experience reporting.
- Develop an inventory consumption rate model using transaction_items dispensing
  data joined to inventory to calculate days-of-supply by product and store.
- Introduce a supplier and purchase order table to enable end-to-end procurement
  cost and lead time analysis, supporting better reorder point calibration.
- Analyse the correlation between clinical service participation and prescription
  refill adherence rates to quantify the patient retention value of the clinical
  programme.
