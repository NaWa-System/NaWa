# Grönmark Livs AB
## Q1 2024 Sales Performance Report
**Organisation:** Grönmark Livs AB  
**Period:** Q1 2024 (January – March 2024)  
**Prepared by:** Business Intelligence & Reporting Team  

---

## Executive Summary

Grönmark Livs AB recorded solid sales performance across its four stores in Q1 2024, with 23 transactions processed totalling approximately SEK 10,120 in combined revenue. The Stockholm region dominated transaction volume, with the Södermalm and Vasastan stores collectively accounting for the majority of activity.

The click-and-collect channel showed strong traction, driven primarily by business members placing bulk orders. Catering Lunden AB and Frisk & Nyttig HB were the top-spending accounts and warrant dedicated account management attention.

March was the busiest month with 9 transactions. Private-label products featured consistently across transactions, reflecting good shelf penetration. Discount usage was targeted and applied mainly on chicken breast lines.

Two transactions were recorded from non-members. Growing member conversion among repeat non-member shoppers should be a Q2 priority.

## Report Sections

### 1. Transaction Volume by Store and Month
Södermalm led all stores with 8 transactions. Monthly volume was 7 in January, 7 in February, and 9 in March. End-of-quarter acceleration tied to increased household member activity and spring product availability.

### 2. Revenue by Channel
Click-and-collect averaged ~SEK 970 per basket vs ~SEK 270 for in-store. Business members drove this gap through bulk orders. Expanding click-and-collect among household members is a key growth lever.

### 3. Top Products by Quantity Sold
Grönmark Chicken Breast led by units. Whole Milk and Organic Tomatoes were the most frequent household purchases. Smoked Salmon and Olive Oil concentrated in click-and-collect baskets. Bakery range maintained steady in-store velocity.

### 4. Member vs Non-Member Sales
91% of transactions linked to members. Business members drove highest basket values. Anna Karlsson and Maria Lindberg were the most frequent household shoppers. Two non-member transactions recorded.

### 5. Discount Usage
Discounts applied to Grönmark Chicken Breast (10–15% for bulk B2B) and Sourdough Loaf (5% in one transaction). No broad promotional discounting observed. Discount exposure modest relative to total revenue.
