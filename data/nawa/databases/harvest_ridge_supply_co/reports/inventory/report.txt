EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
============================================================

This report provides a comprehensive review of inventory positions and procurement activity at Harvest Ridge Supply Co. for the first quarter of 2024. The quarter was characterized by heavy inbound procurement to build stock ahead of the spring planting season, with purchase orders issued to all seven active suppliers across January, February, and March. A total of 22 purchase orders were placed during the period, with 21 received and one remaining in pending status as of the close of the quarter.

Inventory management during Q1 reflected the seasonal demand profile of the business. High-velocity crop input items - particularly Corn Seed Hybrid H200, Urea Fertilizer, and Glyphosate Herbicide - were restocked multiple times across the quarter to meet accelerating order demand. Despite this, the Hydraulic Filter Kit ended Q1 below its defined reorder threshold, representing the most acute stock risk heading into Q2. Insecticide MaxGuard also approached its reorder level by quarter end.

Supplier reliability was strong across the quarter, with all received purchase orders fulfilled without recorded discrepancies. Pioneer AgriSeed Corp. was the most active supplier by order frequency and total procurement value. Great Plains Feed Co. provided consistent and timely deliveries of livestock feed, supporting steady demand from rancher and commercial farm customers throughout the quarter.

Heading into Q2, inventory strategy should focus on expediting the outstanding pending order for Insecticide MaxGuard from ShieldChem Agricultural, proactively assessing whether additional safety stock is warranted for Corn Seed Hybrid H200, and conducting a formal reorder point review for the Farm Equipment Accessories category.

APPENDIX / NOTES
============================================================

ASSUMPTIONS
- Quantity on hand in the inventory table reflects end-of-quarter positions after all Q1 sales and received purchase orders have been applied.
- Procurement spend is calculated as quantity_ordered multiplied by unit_cost for each purchase order row.
- The pending purchase order (PO #22) has not been received and is excluded from inventory on-hand figures.
- All purchase orders with status 'received' are assumed to have been fully delivered at the specified unit cost with no partial deliveries or discrepancies.
- Reorder levels in the products table represent the minimum quantity on hand before a replenishment order should be placed.

KNOWN LIMITATIONS
- The schema does not track inventory adjustments for shrinkage, damage, or write-offs; actual on-hand quantities may differ from recorded values.
- No lead time data is captured for suppliers, limiting the ability to calculate precise reorder trigger dates.
- The inventory table stores a single current snapshot rather than a time series; historical daily stock levels cannot be reconstructed.
- No warehouse location or bin-level tracking is included; all inventory is treated as a single location.

RECOMMENDED NEXT ANALYSIS
- Implement a supplier lead time field in purchase_orders to enable forward-looking reorder point calculations.
- Conduct a formal ABC analysis of SKUs by revenue contribution and stock turn rate.
- Develop a Q2 procurement plan aligned with Q1 sales velocity data, with particular emphasis on seed and fertilizer categories.
- Explore dual-sourcing arrangements for Pioneer AgriSeed Corp. and NutriGrow Fertilizers LLC.
- Add an inventory transaction log table to capture daily movements and enable trend analysis of stock levels over time.
