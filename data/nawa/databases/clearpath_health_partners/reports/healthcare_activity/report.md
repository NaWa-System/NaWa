# Outpatient Clinical Activity & Revenue Report — Q1–Q2 2024

**Organisation:** ClearPath Health Partners
**Reporting Period:** January 1, 2024 – June 30, 2024
**Report Type:** Healthcare Activity
**Prepared By:** Analytics & Reporting Team
**Date of Issue:** July 2024

---

## Executive Summary

This report presents an overview of outpatient clinical activity and billing performance across all five ClearPath Health Partners clinic locations during the first half of 2024. The period covers 35 scheduled appointments, of which 31 (88.6%) were completed successfully. Two appointments resulted in no-shows and two in cancellations.

Total billed revenue across the network exceeded $7,700, with collected amounts of approximately $5,900, reflecting payer mix dynamics including Medicare reimbursement adjustments and outstanding Medicaid and self-pay balances. Diagnostic services — particularly cardiac procedures — generated the highest per-appointment revenue, while preventive care drove the highest appointment volume.

Chronic disease management, principally cardiovascular disease and type 2 diabetes, represents the most recurring clinical workload. Approximately 71% of completed appointments generated follow-up requirements, reflecting active longitudinal care relationships. The organisation is well-positioned entering H2 2024 with stable patient volumes and a growing base of returning patients.

Accounts receivable attention is recommended for three Pending Medicaid claims and two partially collected self-pay billing records, representing a combined exposure of approximately $755.

## Section 1: Appointment Volume & Status by Month

Appointment volumes increased gradually across H1 2024. January and February each recorded five appointments, with volumes rising in March and April to six each, reflecting stronger patient throughput entering the spring period. No-show events occurred in March and May, while cancellations were recorded in April and June.

The 88.6% completion rate is strong relative to industry benchmarks, though the no-show rate warrants monitoring to protect clinician utilisation and slot revenue. Urban clinic locations — particularly ClearPath Central (Columbus) — drove the majority of appointment volume.



---

## Section 2: Revenue & Collections by Payer Type

Medicare represented the dominant payer by both volume and billed revenue, but reimbursement adjustments consistently reduced collected amounts to approximately 80% of billed rates. Private Insurance payers demonstrated the highest collection efficiency, with most claims settled at 100% of billed amounts. Three Medicaid billing records remain Pending, and two self-pay appointments have only partially collected balances.

The organisation's payer mix reflects its patient demographic, with Medicare concentration linked to the significant number of older patients managing chronic cardiac and metabolic conditions.



---

## Section 3: Service Category Performance

Four service categories were active during the period: Consultation, Preventive, Diagnostic, and Specialty. Diagnostic services commanded the highest standard fees, with cardiac stress tests billed at $420 per visit and ECGs at $300. Preventive care — well-child checks, annual wellness exams, and immunisations — recorded the highest appointment volume. Specialty visits (Orthopedics, Endocrinology) generated strong per-visit revenue. Follow-up consultations were the most frequently recurring appointment type among chronic disease patients.



---

## Section 4: Clinic Location Activity Comparison

ClearPath Central (Columbus) recorded the highest appointment volume, driven by concentrated cardiac and chronic disease patient needs. ClearPath Southbrook (Cincinnati) demonstrated strong cardiology and primary care throughput. ClearPath Westside (Dayton) maintained consistent paediatric volumes. ClearPath Lakeview (Toledo) recorded the lowest activity with two orthopedic appointments, suggesting potential capacity expansion or outreach opportunity.



---

## Section 5: Patient Outcome Summary & Follow-Up Demand

Chronic disease management — coronary artery disease (I25.10) and type 2 diabetes mellitus (E11.x) — constitutes the dominant clinical workload. The majority of outcomes were classified as Stable or Improving, suggesting effective care management protocols. One acute cardiac event (I21.9) was escalated appropriately via referral. Preventive and wellness visits were predominantly classified as Healthy with no follow-up requirement.



---

## Section 6: Accounts Receivable & Billing Status

Of 31 billing records issued during H1 2024, the majority have reached Paid status. Three Pending records (Medicaid) and two Partial records (Self-Pay) represent outstanding balances requiring follow-up. The average time from appointment to billing date is approximately two to three business days, indicating efficient claims processing. Private Insurance and Medicare claims are generally resolved promptly.

## Appendix & Notes

### Assumptions
- Medicare reimbursement modelled at ~80% of billed rates per standard CMS fee schedules.
- Medicaid reimbursements modelled at ~80% of billed amounts where paid.
- No-show and cancelled appointments excluded from billing records.
- Follow-up requirements based on clinician-recorded outcome data at the indexed appointment.

### Known Limitations
- Dataset covers 35 appointments across 12 patients; broader statistical conclusions should be drawn with caution.
- No cost-side data available; margin analysis is not possible with the current schema.
- Insurance contract-level fee schedules are approximated.
- Appointment slot capacity not modelled; true utilisation rates cannot be calculated.

### Recommended Next Analysis
- Introduce cost data to enable profitability analysis by clinic and service line.
- Build patient cohort retention model to identify patients overdue for follow-up.
- Expand to full-year 2024 for H1 vs. H2 seasonal comparison.
- Analyse no-show root causes by demographics, payer type, and scheduling patterns.
- Model revenue impact of converting pending and partial billing records to fully collected status.
