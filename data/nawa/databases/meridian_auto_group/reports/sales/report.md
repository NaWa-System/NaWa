# Q1 2024 Vehicle Sales Performance Report

**Organisation:** Meridian Auto Group  
**Reporting Period:** January 1, 2024 – March 31, 2024  
**Prepared by:** Business Intelligence & Reporting Team  
**Date Issued:** April 2024

---

## Executive Summary

Meridian Auto Group closed the first quarter of 2024 with solid sales activity across all five locations, with 20 vehicles retailed between January and March. Total vehicle sales revenue exceeded $555,000, supported by a healthy mix of Economy, Mid-Range, and Premium segment transactions. The quarter demonstrated progressive momentum, with the majority of units sold during March as seasonal demand improved and the online reservation channel continued to drive qualified leads into showrooms.

Gross profit performance was positive across the portfolio, with the Premium segment — anchored by the Mercedes-Benz C-Class and Lexus RX 350 at Nashville and Birmingham — generating the strongest per-unit margins. Finance and insurance (F&I) product attachment rates also showed improvement in Q1, with a meaningful share of sales carrying at least one supplemental product, most commonly Standard Auto Loan financing and Extended Powertrain Warranty coverage.

Trade-in acquisition remained an important inventory pipeline channel, with ten trade-ins accepted during the quarter. Several high-quality trade-ins represent strong reconditioning-and-resale opportunities entering Q2. While overall performance is encouraging, attention is warranted for Economy segment vehicles showing extended days-in-inventory at Chattanooga and Nashville.

## Report Sections

### 1. Monthly Sales Volume & Revenue Trend

Vehicle retail activity accelerated across the quarter. January saw 4 units sold, February added 5, and March delivered 11 units — representing the strongest single month of Q1. Revenue followed this pattern, with March accounting for the majority of quarterly aggregate revenue. This monthly progression is a positive leading indicator heading into Q2.



---

### 2. Revenue & Gross Profit by Segment

The Mid-Range segment drove the highest volume at 10 units. Premium contributed 4 units but generated the largest per-unit revenue and margin, particularly through the Mercedes-Benz C-Class ($39,500) and Lexus RX 350 ($43,500). Economy units sold quickly but at thinner margins. The current segment mix is well-balanced for profitability management.



---

### 3. Location Performance Comparison

All five locations completed sales in Q1. Nashville and Atlanta North were top performers by revenue. Birmingham excelled in Premium unit sales. Columbia showed a consistent mid-range and premium mix. Chattanooga led Economy unit conversions. No location significantly underperformed, and Columbia's trade-in volume signals strong customer equity for Q2 upsell opportunities.



---

### 4. Finance & Insurance (F&I) Product Performance

The Standard Auto Loan was the most widely attached product across Q1. Extended Powertrain Warranty was the second most common, particularly strong on certified vehicles. GAP Insurance and Tire & Wheel Protection showed moderate attachment. Credit Life Insurance had limited take-up, suggesting positioning adjustments are needed. The Lexus RX 350 transaction exemplified best-in-class F&I packaging with three products attached.



---

### 5. Salesperson Performance

Five salespersons were active across the network in Q1. Dale Pruitt (Nashville) achieved the highest per-unit revenue. Tom Archer (Atlanta North) led in unit volume with 4 closings. Rachel Kim (Columbia) demonstrated consistent Premium and Mid-Range performance. All salespersons performed within an acceptable range; however, variance in F&I attachment per deal indicates a coaching opportunity.



---

### 6. Customer Acquisition Channel Analysis

Online channels accounted for approximately 40% of Q1 customers. Walk-In and Referral each represented roughly 30%. Referral customers carried the highest average sale price with no direct acquisition cost, making this the most efficient channel. These findings support continued investment in the referral rewards programme and digital lead funnel optimisation.



---

### 7. Trade-In Acquisition Summary

Ten trade-in vehicles were accepted in Q1 with a combined acquired value of approximately $147,000. Conditions ranged from Fair to Excellent, with two Excellent units — a Mercedes-Benz E-Class and a Lexus ES 350 — representing premium resale prospects. Average trade-in mileage was approximately 74,700 miles. The trade-in pipeline continues to serve as a cost-effective inventory replenishment channel relative to auction sourcing.

## Appendix & Notes

### Assumptions
- Gross profit = sale_price – (acquisition_cost + reconditioning_cost) per vehicle.
- F&I commission totals are sourced directly from sale_finance.commission.
- Acquisition channel attribution is recorded at first customer contact.
- Days-in-inventory is recorded at point of sale.

### Known Limitations
- 20 transactions represent a limited sample; location and salesperson rankings are indicative.
- Trade-in resale outcomes are not yet captured and will affect future profitability analysis.
- Walk-in attribution may undercount digital pre-visit touchpoints.

### Recommended Next Analysis
- Build a rolling 12-month sales trend once additional quarters are available.
- Analyse trade-in resale performance including reconditioning cost variances.
- Develop a salesperson F&I attachment scorecard by product type.
- Model the relationship between days-in-inventory and gross margin discount.
- Identify repeat customer activity to assess retention rates.
