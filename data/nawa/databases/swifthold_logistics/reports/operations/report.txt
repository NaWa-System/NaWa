EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
------------------

SwiftHold Logistics experienced a period of sustained operational activity across
both distribution centers during Q1 2024, processing outbound shipments for five
active clients and managing inbound receipts totaling over 19,000 units across the
quarter. The Columbus DC (OH) handled the majority of outbound volume, driven
primarily by high-frequency clients in the apparel and beauty segments, while the
Reno DC (NV) supported clients in the nutrition and outdoor equipment verticals.

Outbound order volumes showed a consistent upward trend from January through March,
with Brightline Beauty and PixelWear Apparel posting the most significant
month-on-month growth in units shipped. Returns volumes increased modestly across
the quarter, consistent with typical post-holiday return tail patterns in January
and a slight uptick in March that warrants monitoring. The overall restock rate for
returned units remained healthy, averaging above 80% across all clients.

Inventory levels declined steadily across most SKUs through the quarter, indicating
strong sell-through rates. However, several clients — particularly Verde Nutrition
and PixelWear Apparel — may need to accelerate inbound replenishment cycles in Q2
to avoid potential stockout risk.

Overall, Q1 2024 demonstrated SwiftHold's ability to scale outbound throughput
without a proportional increase in operational exceptions. The operations team
should focus Q2 planning on returns trend monitoring, proactive client inventory
reviews, and carrier performance benchmarking.

SECTION 5: RETURNS PROCESSING AND RESTOCK PERFORMANCE
------------------------------------------------------

Returns processing activity was moderate across Q1 2024, with 15 return events
logged totaling 793 units returned. The overall restock rate of approximately 81.8%

The operations team should monitor disposal rates by client and SKU in Q2 to

SECTION 6: CARRIER AND SERVICE LEVEL MIX
-----------------------------------------

Three primary carriers were active across SwiftHold's outbound network in Q1 2024:
FedEx, UPS, and USPS. UPS Ground was the dominant service for high-volume clients
such as Brightline Beauty and Verde Nutrition. USPS Priority was used exclusively
by PixelWear Apparel, appropriate for lightweight garment SKUs. TrekMate Outdoors
utilized FedEx Express for all shipments, reflecting premium delivery expectations.

This carrier mix appears well-aligned to client profiles and shipping zone
distributions. A formal carrier cost-per-shipment analysis is recommended for Q2
negotiations.
