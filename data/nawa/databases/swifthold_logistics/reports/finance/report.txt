EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
------------------

SwiftHold Logistics generated total billed revenue of approximately $76,010 across
five active clients during Q1 2024, reflecting a broadly stable quarter with modest
growth in fulfillment fee revenue driven by rising outbound volumes in the beauty
and apparel segments. Monthly billing records across January, February, and March
show consistent revenue contribution from all active accounts, with no client
recording a significant month-on-month billing decline.

Fulfillment fees represented the largest component of total billed revenue,
consistent with the company's usage-based model where outbound order volume is the
primary cost driver for clients. PixelWear Apparel and Brightline Beauty emerged as
the two highest-billing clients by total quarterly revenue. All January and February
invoices are recorded as Paid, which is a positive indicator of client payment
health and relationship stability.

A notable concern entering Q2 is the Pending payment status of three March billing
records, representing approximately $16,555 in outstanding receivables that will
require follow-up. Management should also evaluate whether value-added service
adoption can be expanded across clients currently billing zero in that category, as
this represents a clear upsell opportunity within the existing account base.

SECTION 5: STORAGE FEE REVENUE AND INVENTORY CORRELATION
----------------------------------------------------------

Storage fee revenue declined modestly across Q1 2024 as aggregate inventory levels
trended downward at both distribution centers. Pallet occupancy data from inventory
snapshots confirms that pallets occupied decreased across most SKUs from January
through March, directly correlating with observed storage fee declines per client.

Verde Nutrition consistently generated the highest storage fees due to its
pallet-dominant inventory profile, while Brightline Beauty generated the lowest
storage fees relative to its total billing. If clients replenish aggressively in
Q2 to protect against stockout risk, storage fee revenue could recover and
potentially exceed Q1 levels.
