# Commercial Insurance Broking — Sales & Commission Income Report
## Q1/Q2 2024 (January – April 2024)

**Organisation:** Meridian Cover Solutions Ltd
**Report Type:** Sales & Commission Income
**Prepared by:** Management Information Team
**Date:** May 2024

---

## Executive Summary

This report presents a consolidated review of Meridian Cover Solutions Ltd's sales activity and commission income performance for the period January through April 2024. The period captures the firm's peak renewal season as well as a healthy volume of new business placements, collectively driving total brokerage income that is materially ahead of the equivalent period in the prior year.

Gross written premium (GWP) placed across Meridian's insurer panel during the period reached approximately £236,300, generating commission income of approximately £29,693 before arrangement fees. When arrangement fees are included, total brokerage income for the period stands at approximately £32,473. The Commercial Property and Fleet Motor product lines remain the largest contributors to GWP, together accounting for roughly half of all premium placed during the period.

New business activity was concentrated in March and April 2024, with four new policies placed across cyber liability, public liability, contractors all risk, and employers liability lines. The North West region continued to generate the highest individual policy values, driven by Castleway Logistics Group's fleet programme and a growing technology and healthcare client base.

Looking ahead, the pipeline of April renewals and the onboarding of three new clients in Q1 2024 position Meridian well for continued income growth into Q2. Management attention is recommended on the lapsed account of Coldwell Fabrications Ltd, whose property policy was not renewed, representing an avoidable GWP leakage of £8,900.

## 1. Commission Income by Month

Commission income was strongest in January 2024, reflecting the concentration of major renewal dates at the start of the calendar year. January generated total income of approximately £14,537, driven primarily by the Sandhurst Hotels Group and Greenway Property Developments renewals. February saw income of approximately £7,382, underpinned by the Castleway Logistics fleet renewal. March delivered approximately £7,867 as new business placements came through for Ridgeline Civil Engineering and Apex Fleet Management. April showed emerging momentum from new business activity, with Lynbrook Care Services placing two policies simultaneously.

The monthly income trend reflects the typical seasonality of a commercial brokerage with a January-heavy renewal book.

---

## 2. GWP and Income by Product Line

Commercial Property was the dominant product line during the period, accounting for approximately £100,900 of GWP across five policies. Fleet Motor ranked second with £51,300 of GWP across two large fleet programmes, generating strong commission income at a 15% panel rate. Professional Indemnity placed approximately £23,300 of GWP across four policies, reflecting Meridian's growing presence in professional services and technology sectors.

SME Package policies contributed consistent, low-maintenance income. Cyber Liability is an emerging product line with two policies now active and growth expected as the technology sector client base expands.

## 4. Commission Rate Analysis by Insurer

Northgate Underwriters delivered the highest commission rate at 15.00%, applied to the two fleet motor policies totalling £51,300 GWP. Pinnacle Indemnity Plc and Broadstone Commercial performed at 13.50–14.00%, in line with targets. Redwood Specialty Markets (Cyber) achieved 11.00%, the lowest on the active panel, though this is expected for a specialist cyber product.

Avanta Commercial Insurance remains the highest-volume insurer by GWP, processing over £100,000 of property premium at a 12.50% rate. Croft Re Solutions remains on Restricted status; no new business was placed with this insurer during the period.

---

## 5. New Business vs Renewal Income Split

Of the 20 commission ledger entries, 14 relate to renewals, 5 to new business placements, and 1 to a mid-term adjustment. Renewal income accounted for approximately £27,400 of total brokerage income; new business contributed approximately £3,924; the mid-term adjustment a further £230.

The renewal-to-new-business income ratio of roughly 87:13 is consistent with a mature brokerage book. The four new policies placed in March and April 2024 represent a positive directional signal toward the strategic objective of growing new business contribution.

---

## 6. Client Sector and Regional Breakdown

By sector, Hospitality and Logistics clients generated the highest total GWP. Sandhurst Hotels Group alone accounted for £45,700 of premium across two policies. The Technology and Professional Services sectors are growing contributors with five active policies. By region, the Midlands generated the broadest policy count, the North West the highest individual policy values, and Yorkshire a mix of long-standing large accounts and smaller SME clients.

Healthcare, represented by Lynbrook Care Services, is a newly active sector following two policy placements in April 2024.

## Appendix & Notes

### Assumptions
- Commission rates in the ledger are consistent with current panel agreements.
- GWP figures represent annual premiums without pro-rata adjustment.
- Arrangement fees are recognised at policy inception.
- Coldwell Fabrications is excluded from active portfolio metrics.
- The Apex Fleet mid-term adjustment reflects a legitimate fleet vehicle addition.

### Known Limitations
- Report covers January–April 2024 only; full-year data unavailable.
- Commission income is recognised on placement date, not cash-received basis.
- No cost or overhead data is included; net profitability cannot be calculated.
- Pipeline and quoted-but-not-placed business is not captured.

### Recommended Next Analysis
- Full-year income projection using renewal schedule expiry dates.
- Rolling 12-month commission rate trend analysis by insurer.
- Client lifetime value segmentation using tenure and cumulative commission data.
- New business pipeline report once quote management functionality is introduced.
- Retention cost-benefit analysis for at-risk SME clients.
