========================================================
EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
========================================================

This report presents a consolidated review of Meridian Cover Solutions Ltd's sales activity and commission income performance for the period January through April 2024. The period captures the firm's peak renewal season as well as a healthy volume of new business placements, collectively driving total brokerage income that is materially ahead of the equivalent period in the prior year.

Gross written premium (GWP) placed across Meridian's insurer panel during the period reached approximately £236,300, generating commission income of approximately £29,693 before arrangement fees. When arrangement fees are included, total brokerage income for the period stands at approximately £32,473. The Commercial Property and Fleet Motor product lines remain the largest contributors to GWP, together accounting for roughly half of all premium placed.

New business activity was concentrated in March and April 2024, with four new policies placed across cyber liability, public liability, contractors all risk, and employers liability lines. The North West region continued to generate the highest individual policy values, driven by Castleway Logistics Group's fleet programme and a growing technology and healthcare client base.

Looking ahead, the pipeline of April renewals and the onboarding of three new clients in Q1 2024 position Meridian well for continued income growth into Q2. Management attention is recommended on the lapsed account of Coldwell Fabrications Ltd, whose property policy was not renewed, representing an avoidable GWP leakage of £8,900.

========================================================
SECTION 1: COMMISSION INCOME BY MONTH
========================================================

Commission income was strongest in January 2024, reflecting the concentration of major renewal dates at the start of the calendar year. January generated total income of approximately £14,537, driven primarily by the Sandhurst Hotels Group and Greenway Property Developments renewals. February saw income of approximately £7,382, underpinned by the Castleway Logistics fleet renewal. March delivered approximately £7,867 as new business placements came through for Ridgeline Civil Engineering and Apex Fleet Management. April showed emerging momentum from new business activity, with Lynbrook Care Services placing two policies simultaneously, contributing £1,790 in income.

The monthly income trend reflects the typical seasonality of a commercial brokerage with a January-heavy renewal book.

========================================================
SECTION 2: GWP AND INCOME BY PRODUCT LINE
========================================================

Commercial Property was the dominant product line during the period, accounting for approximately £100,900 of GWP across five policies placed with Avanta Commercial Insurance and Croft Re Solutions. Fleet Motor ranked second with £51,300 of GWP across two large fleet programmes (Castleway Logistics and Apex Fleet Management), generating strong commission income at a 15% panel rate.

Professional Indemnity placed approximately £23,300 of GWP across four policies, reflecting Meridian's growing presence in the professional services and technology sectors. SME Package policies contributed consistent and low-maintenance income. Cyber Liability is an emerging product line with two policies now active and growth expected as the technology sector client base expands.

========================================================
SECTION 3: INCOME BY ACCOUNT MANAGER
========================================================

Sarah Whitfield (Midlands, Commercial) generated the highest total brokerage income of the period, supported by the Thornfield Manufacturing and Ashbrook Legal accounts alongside the Sandhurst Hotels renewal. James Okafor (North West, Commercial) ranks second, anchored by the Castleway Logistics fleet programme.

Rachel Pinto (Yorkshire, Commercial) managed the Sandhurst Hotels commercial property renewal and the Pennine Retail SME package, delivering solid income from established long-tenure clients. Among the SME team, Daniel Hewitt (Midlands) demonstrated the strongest new business conversion rate. Priya Sharma (North West, SME) successfully renewed NovaTech's cyber and professional indemnity portfolio. Tom Bradshaw (Yorkshire, SME) managed the lapsed Coldwell Fabrications account and should be supported with a retention strategy.

========================================================
SECTION 4: COMMISSION RATE ANALYSIS BY INSURER
========================================================

Northgate Underwriters delivered the highest commission rate at 15.00%, applied to the two fleet motor policies totalling £51,300 GWP. Pinnacle Indemnity Plc and Broadstone Commercial performed at 13.50–14.00%, in line with targets. Redwood Specialty Markets (Cyber) achieved 11.00%, the lowest on the active panel, though this is expected for specialist cyber coverage.

Avanta Commercial Insurance remains the highest-volume insurer by GWP, processing over £100,000 of property premium at 12.50%. Croft Re Solutions remains on Restricted status and no new business was placed with this insurer during the period.

========================================================
SECTION 5: NEW BUSINESS VS RENEWAL INCOME SPLIT
========================================================

Of the 20 commission ledger entries recorded in the period, 14 relate to renewals, 5 to new business placements, and 1 to a mid-term adjustment. Renewal income accounted for approximately £27,400; new business approximately £3,924; and the mid-term adjustment £230.

The renewal-to-new-business ratio of approximately 87:13 is consistent with a mature brokerage book. The four new policies placed in March and April 2024 represent a positive directional signal toward the firm's strategic objective of growing new business contribution.

========================================================
SECTION 6: CLIENT SECTOR AND REGIONAL BREAKDOWN
========================================================

By sector, Hospitality and Logistics clients generated the highest total GWP. Sandhurst Hotels Group alone accounted for £45,700 of premium across two policies. The Technology and Professional Services sectors are growing contributors with five active policies collectively.

By region, the Midlands generated the broadest policy count, the North West the highest individual policy values, and Yorkshire a mix of long-standing large accounts and smaller SME clients. Healthcare, represented by Lynbrook Care Services, is a newly active sector for the firm following two policy placements in April 2024.

APPENDIX & NOTES
========================================================

ASSUMPTIONS
- Commission rates in the ledger are consistent with current panel agreements as stored.
- GWP figures represent annual premiums; no pro-rata adjustment has been applied.
- Arrangement fees are recognised at policy inception date.
- Coldwell Fabrications Ltd is excluded from active portfolio metrics.
- The Apex Fleet mid-term adjustment reflects a legitimate fleet vehicle addition.

KNOWN LIMITATIONS
- Report covers January–April 2024 only; full-year data is unavailable at this time.
- Commission income is recognised on placement date, not on a cash-received basis.
- No cost or overhead data is included; net profitability cannot be calculated.
- Pipeline and quoted-but-not-placed business is not captured in this report.
