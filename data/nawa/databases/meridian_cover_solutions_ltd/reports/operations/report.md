# Policy Retention, Renewal Performance & Claims Activity Report
## Q1/Q2 2024 (January – April 2024)

**Organisation:** Meridian Cover Solutions Ltd
**Report Type:** Operations
**Prepared by:** Management Information Team
**Date:** May 2024

---

## Executive Summary

This operational report provides Meridian Cover Solutions Ltd's management team with a structured review of policy renewal performance, client retention outcomes, and claims activity across the January to April 2024 period. Together, these three operational dimensions represent the core service delivery indicators that underpin the firm's long-term client relationships and insurer panel reputation.

Across the period, Meridian processed 20 renewal events, of which 17 resulted in a successful retained or new business placement. The overall retention rate for policies due for renewal stands at approximately 93%, which is strong by industry standards and reflects the account management team's proactive engagement strategy. The single non-renewed policy — Coldwell Fabrications Ltd's commercial property cover — is attributable to the client entering a period of reduced trading activity rather than competitive displacement.

On the claims side, 15 claims were registered across the portfolio between January and April 2024, with a total claimed amount of approximately £126,800. Of these, 8 have been settled for a combined settled amount of approximately £84,100, representing an average settlement ratio of approximately 92% of claimed value on closed cases. Seven claims remain open, concentrated in the fleet motor, liability, and professional indemnity lines.

Overall, Meridian's operational performance in Q1/Q2 2024 is satisfactory. Retention is strong, new business additions are healthy, and the claims management process is functioning within normal parameters. However, the volume of open claims entering May 2024 warrants active chase-up with insurer claims teams to ensure prompt resolution and client satisfaction.

## 1. Renewal Performance Summary

Of the 20 events recorded in the period, 14 were classified as renewals, 5 as new business, and 1 as a mid-term adjustment. Of the 15 policies eligible for renewal, 14 were successfully renewed, with all retained policies recording a retention_flag of TRUE. The sole non-renewal was the Coldwell Fabrications Ltd commercial property policy. The renewal cycle was busiest in January (5 renewals), followed by March (4 renewals) and February (3 renewals), consistent with the portfolio's January calendar-year inception skew.

---

## 2. GWP Movement on Renewals

Comparing prior-year GWP against renewed GWP across the 14 successfully retained policies reveals a consistent pattern of moderate premium uplifts. The Sandhurst Hotels Group commercial property renewal saw the largest absolute uplift (+£2,500), and Castleway Logistics fleet renewed at an 8.6% increase. Across all renewed policies, aggregate GWP moved from approximately £186,100 to approximately £198,600, an overall uplift of approximately 6.7%. This organic GWP growth directly increases commission income on a like-for-like basis.

---

## 3. Claims Frequency and Volume by Product Line

Fleet Motor generated the highest claims frequency (3 events, ~£56,000 combined claimed value). Commercial Property recorded 3 claims totalling ~£29,500, all now settled. Liability lines recorded 4 claims totalling ~£20,200, with 3 open. Professional Indemnity has one open claim for £15,000. Cyber Liability recorded one settled claim at £18,500 gross settlement against a £22,000 claim. SME Package produced the lowest average claim values (2 settled claims, ~£3,900 combined).

---

## 4. Open Claims Analysis

As at end of April 2024, seven claims remain open with combined outstanding exposure of approximately £61,500:

- **Fleet Motor:** 2 claims (£24,800 combined) — Apex Fleet vehicle theft and Castleway Logistics vehicle collision
- **Public Liability:** 2 claims (£9,700 combined) — Sandhurst Hotels slip and Ridgeline Civil Engineering injury
- **Professional Indemnity:** 1 claim (£15,000) — Ashbrook Legal professional negligence
- **Employers Liability:** 1 claim (£3,800) — Lynbrook Care Services employee injury
- **Third Party Property Damage:** 1 claim (£3,200) — Ridgeline Civil Engineering contractors

Account managers should maintain active contact on all open matters. The Ashbrook Legal claim warrants particular attention given its sensitivity and value.

---

## 5. Settlement Ratio and Claims Outcome Analysis

Of the 8 settled claims, total claimed value was approximately £47,700 and total settled value approximately £44,600, yielding an average settlement ratio of approximately 93.5%. Commercial property claims settled in full or near-full. The cyber liability claim settled at 84.1% of claimed value, within expected parameters. No claim was declined outright by any panel insurer during the period. These ratios are informative for client expectation management at renewal discussions.

---

## 6. Client Retention and Lapse Risk Assessment

The single lapsed client, Coldwell Fabrications Ltd, has been reclassified in the client record. The associated property policy expiry results in the loss of approximately £890 per annum in commission income. Account manager Tom Bradshaw (Yorkshire, SME) should initiate a reconnection to assess reinstatement viability in H2 2024.

Beyond this lapse, no other clients are currently flagged as at-risk. However, single-policy clients such as Harwick Catering Services and Ironbridge Digital Agency should be prioritised for cross-sell conversations to deepen relationship stickiness.

## 7. Claims Activity by Region and Account Manager

The North West region recorded the highest number of claim events (6), reflecting the region's larger and more complex fleet and commercial risks. The Midlands recorded 5 claims, with the Thornfield Manufacturing fire damage and Ashbrook Legal professional negligence claims the most significant by value. Yorkshire recorded 4 claims. James Okafor (North West) is managing the most active open claims caseload and should receive prioritised claims team support. No account manager recorded zero claims across their managed portfolio.

## Appendix & Notes

### Assumptions
- Retention rate calculated on policies with a renewal event due January–April 2024 only.
- New business policies (NULL prior_gwp) excluded from GWP growth calculations.
- Settled claims are those with claim_status = 'Settled'; open claims are claim_status = 'Open'.
- Mid-term adjustment excluded from formal renewal count.
- Claims registered after 30 April 2024 are outside scope.

### Known Limitations
- No insurer reserve data available; open liability estimates are based on claimed amounts only.
- No claims handling timeline data captured; service efficiency cannot be assessed.
- Formal loss ratio or combined ratio calculation is not possible from Meridian's internal data alone.
- No client satisfaction data is available in the current schema.

### Recommended Next Analysis
- Add claims timeline data to enable handling KPI reporting (days to acknowledgement, days to settlement).
- Introduce an at-risk flag to the clients or policies table for proactive retention monitoring.
- Build a rolling 12-month claims frequency analysis by product line to support panel review and pricing strategy.
- Develop a cross-sell opportunity report for single-policy clients by sector.
- Commission an indicative insurer-level loss ratio estimate to inform the next panel review discussion.
